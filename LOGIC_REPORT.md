# Categorization Logic Report

This document outlines the plain-language logic used to score and categorize students in the Smart Candidate Screening System.

## The Goal
To create a 100-point scoring system that accurately reflects a candidate's "placement readiness" based on their academic foundation, technical skills, practical experience (projects), industry exposure (internships), and supplementary certifications.

## Weighting Scheme (Out of 100)
1. **CGPA (35 points max)**: Academic performance is historically the heaviest filter for campus placements.
2. **Technical Skills (25 points max)**: Direct indicator of job readiness.
3. **Projects (20 points max)**: Proof of practical application.
4. **Internships (15 points max)**: Real-world industry exposure.
5. **Certifications (5 points max)**: Bonus points for extra effort.

## Categorization Thresholds
- **Strong (75 - 100 points)**: These candidates have an excellent mix of high grades and practical skills.
- **Average (50 - 74 points)**: These candidates might have good grades but lack projects/internships, or vice versa.
- **Needs Improvement (0 - 49 points)**: These candidates have very low grades or lack basic technical skills and projects.

## Detailed Scoring Breakdown

### CGPA
- 9.0+ = 35 points
- 8.0 - 8.99 = 28 points
- 7.0 - 7.99 = 20 points
- 6.0 - 6.99 = 12 points
- Below 6.0 = 5 points

### Technical Skills
- 5+ skills = 25 points
- 4 skills = 20 points
- 3 skills = 15 points
- 2 skills = 8 points
- 1 skill = 4 points

### Projects
- 4+ projects = 20 points
- 3 projects = 15 points
- 2 projects = 10 points
- 1 project = 5 points

### Internships
- 2+ internships = 15 points
- 1 internship = 10 points

### Certifications
- 2+ certifications = 5 points
- 1 certification = 3 points

*Note: Missing data, blank fields, or explicit 'None' values are scored as 0 for that specific field.*
